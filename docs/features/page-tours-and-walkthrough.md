Eleven Trizlink pages carry a **Tour this page** button beside the page title. Press it and a card walks you
through that page, pointing at each part on the real screen while the rest stays dimmed behind it. For the whole
product, open the [walkthrough](https://trizlink.com/walkthrough).

## Which pages have a tour?

Home, the dashboard, short links, the new-link form, bio pages, the bio page editor, the social composer,
analytics, settings, plans and admin. Editing a link has no tour.

## How do I start a page tour?

Press **Tour this page**. From 1024 px wide the button shows its label; narrower, it is the
compass icon alone. A second press closes the tour.

## How do I move through a tour?

**Next** and **Back** step through it, as do the arrow keys while the card has focus. **All steps** lists
every step, and picking one jumps there. **Skip tour**, **Close the tour**, Escape and a press
outside the card all end it. On a phone the card is a sheet along the bottom; a swipe down closes it.

## Is the tour different on a phone?

Yes. Every tour has three step sets: desktop from 1024 px, tablet from 768 px and phone below that, and on the
dashboard that means seven steps on desktop and five on a phone. A step whose part is not on screen is left out.

## How do I replay a tour?

Press **Tour this page** again. Every press starts from step 1, and every tour ends by pointing at that
button.

## What is the hint beside the button?

The first time you open a toured page, the button pulses and a note reads *Take a quick tour of this page.*
It shows once per page and counts as seen the moment it appears — on this browser and, while you are signed
in, on your account, so a second device does not repeat it. After 8 seconds, or your first press, key, scroll
or touch, it goes. It does not come back.

## How does the walkthrough work?

The walkthrough at [trizlink.com/walkthrough](https://trizlink.com/walkthrough) is public and shows Trizlink in
six steps: short links, bio pages, the composer, analytics, AI and plans.
Trizlink has one AI switch for every workspace, separate from your own setting in Settings → AI; while that
switch is off, the AI step is left out and the page says five. Each step shows a real screen at desktop and
phone size.

Move with the step list, **Back** and **Next step**, the arrow keys or a swipe. The address keeps your place:
`?step=4` opens step 4, step 1 has no parameter, and the browser's Back button walks back one step. Any value
outside 2–6 opens step 1. The last step ends on **Create your free account**, or **Open your dashboard** when
you are signed in.

It is linked as **Product walkthrough** in the sidebar, account menu and footer. Sign-up links it too.

The workspace shown is a demo. Its names and numbers are not yours.
