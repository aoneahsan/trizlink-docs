Every Trizlink plan includes AI at one of three levels. It drafts a post, suggests a bio tagline, reads the
numbers on your analytics page, and turns up to 2 minutes of speech into a proposal you check before anything is
written. It runs on our credit, inside monthly allowances your plan sets. Your own Anthropic or OpenAI key lifts
those allowances on every plan.

## What are Spark, Pulse and Surge?

Spark, Pulse and Surge are Trizlink's three AI levels: Spark is quick and light, Pulse has sharper reasoning, and
Surge does the deepest thinking. Free runs at Spark. Team runs at Surge. Pro runs at Pulse and moves up to Surge
once you have paid for it 3 months in a row. You see a level, never a model.

A level is the most you get. When our credit runs low, a request can run a level lower for a while; the
level badge says so, and your level comes back by itself.

## Does my plan limit AI?

Yes. Every plan has two monthly allowances: AI requests for drafts, taglines and insights, and voice requests
for recordings.

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI level | Spark | Pulse · Surge after 3 paid months in a row | Surge |
| AI requests each month | 50 | 500 | 2,000 |
| Voice requests each month | 10 | 100 | 150 |

Without your own key, the count shows before you press. Sometimes our credit cannot pay for a request. The screen then
says *AI is resting until* a stated time, and nothing is counted while it rests. A paid plan can fall back to
our paid key and keep going. Free waits.

## How do I use my own AI key?

Add it from **Settings → AI**. Your own Anthropic or OpenAI key lifts both allowances on every plan; what happens
to the key, and to the count, is on [AI assistance](./ai-features-byok.md#byok).

## How does voice work?

Press the microphone on a post, a bio page or a new link, then say what the post is about, who you are, or where
the link should go. Pressing Start asks for the microphone. A recording runs up to 2 minutes.

You get a proposal: a draft, a tagline with up to 2 text blocks, or a filled-in link form. Nothing is written
until you confirm it, and a new link is created only when you press **Create link**. Keeping a recording counts
nothing. Making its proposal counts one voice request. On the web, where the browser can, your words appear as
you speak.

## How long are recordings kept?

A recording's audio is deleted within 24 hours. One exception: if the storage service cannot be reached, or does
not confirm the deletion when the audio is due to go, it is deleted at a later hourly check.

Its transcripts and the proposal stay until you delete the recording, the workspace it belongs to, or your
account. Every recording is listed under **Your recordings** in Settings → AI, and your account export includes
each one's transcripts and proposal. The [privacy policy](https://trizlink.com/privacy-policy) lists what each
feature sends, and to whom.
