TrizLink custom domains are your own domains connected to TrizLink so your short links use your branding instead of `trizlink.com`. You add a domain, point its DNS to TrizLink, and then create branded short links on it, while managing multiple domains within a single workspace and viewing analytics for each one separately. DNS, SSL, and routing are configured in the domain settings. Custom domains apply to short links; bio pages continue to use `trizlink.com` slugs and do not require a custom domain.

## What you can do

You can add your own domain for branded short links, manage several domains in one workspace, and review per-domain analytics. You configure the DNS, SSL, and routing for each domain in its settings. Setting up a custom domain requires access to your domain's DNS so you can point it at TrizLink.

## Use cases

- **Brand your short links.** Use your own domain so shared links carry your name rather than the default `trizlink.com`.
- **Separate brands or clients.** Manage multiple domains in one workspace to keep different brands or clients on their own branded links.
- **Measure each domain.** View per-domain analytics to compare how links on different domains perform.
- **Reinforce trust.** Share links on a domain your audience recognises, which can feel more familiar than a generic shortener.
- **Consolidate management.** Keep all your domains and their links in a single TrizLink workspace instead of scattered tools.

## How it works

1. Sign in with Google and open the domain settings in your workspace.
2. Add the domain you want to use for branded short links.
3. Point the domain's DNS to TrizLink as instructed, which requires access to your DNS provider.
4. Complete the DNS, SSL, and routing configuration in the domain settings.
5. Once the domain is connected, create short links on it so they use your branding.
6. Open per-domain analytics to track how links on each domain perform.

## Tips

- Make sure you have access to your domain's DNS settings before you begin, since pointing DNS is required.
- Allow time for DNS changes to take effect, as they are not always instant.
- Add and verify one domain at a time so configuration issues are easy to isolate.
- Use per-domain analytics to compare performance when you run more than one branded domain.
- Keep using `trizlink.com` slugs for bio pages, since custom domains are for short links rather than bios.

## FAQ

### What is a custom domain in TrizLink?

It is your own domain connected to TrizLink so your short links are branded with it instead of using `trizlink.com`.

### What do I need to set one up?

You need access to your domain's DNS so you can point it to TrizLink. DNS, SSL, and routing are then configured in the domain settings.

### Can I use more than one domain?

Yes. You can manage multiple domains per workspace and create branded short links on each of them.

### Do I get analytics per domain?

Yes. You can view per-domain analytics so you can compare how links on each domain perform.

### Do bio pages need a custom domain?

No. Bio pages use `trizlink.com` slugs and do not require a custom domain. Custom domains apply to short links.

### Why isn't my domain working yet?

Custom domains require DNS to be pointed correctly and DNS changes can take time to propagate. Double-check the DNS, SSL, and routing settings, then allow time before testing again.

### Are custom domains free?

TrizLink is a free platform. You do, however, need your own domain and DNS access, which you arrange with your domain provider, since the domain itself is yours.
