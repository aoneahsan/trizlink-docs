A link-in-bio builder with a free tier and two yearly plans that add analytics, forms and a custom domain. This page is for you if you are on Taplink now and want to see what moving takes. For who each product suits better, read [Trizlink and Taplink](https://trizlink.com/compare/taplink).

## Moving your page across {#steps}

1. **Write down what is on your Taplink page.** There is no importer for a page from another product, so this is the list you build from.
2. **[Create a Trizlink account](/getting-started/create-account).** Sign-in is with Google, and a workspace is made for you.
3. **Build the page from [blocks](/features/link-in-bio#blocks).** Pick a [layout](/features/link-in-bio#layouts) first, then add the links, the email form and the contact form you need.
4. **[Publish it and choose its address](/features/link-in-bio#publishing).** Open it on a phone before you change anything else.
5. **Put the new address in your profiles.** Leave the Taplink page up until the new one has been in place for a while, because old posts still point at it.

## What does not come with you {#stays}

- **Your subscribers and messages.** They stay with Taplink. This guide has no import step for them.
- **Your page history.** Views and taps start counting on the new page. See [limits](/features/link-in-bio#limits) for how a page is counted.
