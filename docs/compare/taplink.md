Taplink builds link-in-bio pages with a free tier and two yearly plans that add analytics, forms and a custom domain. For selling digital products and sending automated emails from the page for under a hundred dollars a year, or for scheduling when blocks appear, it has the tools. If click analytics and a contact form should come before any payment, Trizlink includes both free, and the bio page is one of three things on a single subscription. [Trizlink and Taplink](https://trizlink.com/compare/taplink).

## Taplink to a Trizlink bio page {#steps}

1. **List every block and where it sits.** Taplink pages do not import, so this list is what you rebuild from.
2. **[Sign in with Google](/getting-started/create-account)** and open a new page as a draft.
3. **Swap timed blocks for a Countdown.** Trizlink has no scheduled display of blocks. A Countdown block, with its timezone set to yours, the visitor's or UTC, covers the "it starts on Friday" case.
4. **Move the forms.** Taplink's application forms are on Business; here the Contact form [block](/features/link-in-bio#blocks) is on every plan.
5. **Bring your pixels.** Add each pixel under [tracking](/features/tracking-and-utm#pixels) and tick `bio_view`. That attaches it to every bio page in the workspace, because pages have no pixel panel of their own.

## What stays on Taplink {#stays}

- **Digital products and automated emails.** Trizlink does not sell from the page.
- **Your Taplink address and custom domain.** Pages here live on `trizlink.com`; a domain can be [verified](/features/custom-domains#not-live) but not served yet.
- **Click analytics so far.**
