Beacons is a creator page builder: a link in bio plus a store, email tools, a media kit and AI helpers. Creators who pitch brand deals and want rate cards and invoices on the same account are its people, and so is anyone for whom a registered custom domain is worth the Creator price. If what you want beside the bio page is short links and a social calendar rather than a store, or a team of up to 30 on a free plan, Trizlink fits better. [The comparison](https://trizlink.com/compare/beacons).

## Moving the page, keeping the shop {#steps}

1. **Split the page into what sells and what links.** The store, media kit and invoices stay on Beacons. The plain links, the email sign-up and the contact form can move.
2. **[Create your Trizlink account](/getting-started/create-account)** and start a page; it stays a draft until you publish, and drafts never count against your plan.
3. **Link to the shop instead of rebuilding it.** A Link block pointing at your Beacons store keeps sales where the checkout is.
4. **Rebuild the capture blocks.** Beacons lists email collection and a contact form; Trizlink has [both as blocks](/features/link-in-bio#blocks), and contact messages land in an inbox inside the product.
5. **[Publish](/features/link-in-bio#publishing), then invite your manager or editor** to the workspace with a [role](/features/workspaces-and-teams#roles). Roles come with every plan.

## What stays on Beacons {#stays}

- **Your store, media kit and rate cards.**
- **The fan emails it already holds.** There is no import from Beacons here.
- **Your registered custom domain.** Trizlink [verifies a domain](/features/custom-domains#not-live) but does not serve one yet, and bio pages are always on `trizlink.com`.
- **Page analytics to date.** Counting restarts on the new page.
