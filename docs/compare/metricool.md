Metricool is an analytics-led planner priced by brand, with a free plan and a link-in-bio on paid tiers. Competitor profile analysis and unlimited analytics history are reasons to pay for it, and pricing that scales by brand suits an agency billing that way. If links and bio pages are the job, and scheduled posts can wait until publishing is switched on, Trizlink is the closer fit, with passwords and expiry dates on links. [Trizlink and Metricool](https://trizlink.com/compare/metricool).

## What moves from Metricool {#steps}

**Metricool keeps posting and its competitor reports.** Trizlink's publishing is [built and switched off](/features/social-media#not-live).

1. **Rebuild the Starter bio page.** Metricool's link-in-bio starts on a paid tier; a [Trizlink bio page](/features/link-in-bio) is on the free plan, up to 30 published.
2. **[Sign in](/getting-started/create-account)** and make one [workspace](/features/workspaces-and-teams#what-it-owns) per brand if you manage several.
3. **Measure links on their own.** Each short link has its own [click report](/features/analytics), kept 90 days on Free, 365 on Pro and 730 on Team.
4. **Use the new links in posts you still schedule from Metricool.**

## What stays on Metricool {#stays}

- **Competitor analysis and its analytics history.**
- **Scheduled posts, brands and connected accounts.**
- **The approval step on its Advanced plan.**
