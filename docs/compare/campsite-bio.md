Campsite.bio is a bio-link profile tool with a free plan, a seven-dollar Pro tier and organisation plans for teams. People managing many profiles, who like paying $1.50 for each extra one, are its core, and non-profits get a discount. If 14 days of click history on a free plan is too short, Trizlink's keeps 90, and the contact form lands in an inbox inside the product. [How they compare](https://trizlink.com/compare/campsite-bio).

## Moving profiles from Campsite.bio {#steps}

1. **Count your profiles.** Each Campsite profile becomes one Trizlink bio page. Free covers 30 published pages, Pro 100 and Team 500, and drafts are unlimited.
2. **[Sign in](/getting-started/create-account)** and make a [workspace](/features/workspaces-and-teams#what-it-owns) per client if the profiles belong to different people, so their pages and numbers never mix.
3. **Build the first page, then duplicate it.** [Duplicating](/features/link-in-bio#publishing) copies a page as a draft with all its blocks, which is quicker than starting each profile from nothing.
4. **Replace the embedded form.** Campsite embeds Jotform or Typeform. Use the Contact form block instead, and messages arrive in Trizlink.
5. **Invite your organisation's members** with a [role](/features/workspaces-and-teams#inviting) each. They need a Google account to accept.

## What stays on Campsite.bio {#stays}

- **Opt-in subscribers and Jotform or Typeform entries.** They are with Campsite and those services.
- **Custom domains on paid plans.** Trizlink cannot [serve a domain](/features/custom-domains#not-live) yet.
- **The click history so far.**
