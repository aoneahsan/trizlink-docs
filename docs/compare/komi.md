Komi is a creator mini-site with a free Starter tier and a Pro tier for digital products and email campaigns. If you sell digital products at 0% fees and send campaigns from the same place, or YouTube, Spotify and Shopify embeds are the centre of your page, Komi fits. If you want the paid price on the page rather than behind a trial, with short links and social drafts beside the page, Trizlink fits. [Trizlink and Komi](https://trizlink.com/compare/komi).

## Komi to Trizlink {#steps}

1. **Leave products and campaigns on Komi.** Link to your Komi store from the new page so sales stay where they work.
2. **[Sign in with Google](/getting-started/create-account).**
3. **Bring the media.** Trizlink has YouTube, Spotify and Video [blocks](/features/link-in-bio#blocks). There is no Shopify block; link to the shop instead.
4. **Check embeds on your own network.** Spotify and YouTube blocks depend on those services loading for each visitor, and Trizlink cannot make a blocked embed appear.
5. **[Publish](/features/link-in-bio#publishing)** and put the new address in your bio.

## What stays on Komi {#stays}

- **Digital products and email campaigns.**
- **Fan contacts it has collected.**
- **A Pro custom domain.** Custom domains [cannot serve traffic on Trizlink yet](/features/custom-domains#not-live).
