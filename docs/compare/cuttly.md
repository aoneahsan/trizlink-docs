Cuttly is a shortener with link-in-bio pages, team plans and a detailed feature table per plan. Two things are hard to find elsewhere: TRAI tracking headers for SMS sent in India, and a team chat with five roles inside the shortener. If either is part of your work, stay. If 30 links a month on a free account is too few to test anything properly, or you want the social calendar reading the same click reports as the links, Trizlink is the closer fit. [Trizlink and Cuttly](https://trizlink.com/compare/cuttly).

## Cuttly to Trizlink, in order {#steps}

1. **Export your links from Cuttly.** Its pricing table lists bulk tools; find the export in your plan, or list the live links by hand.
2. **[Open a Trizlink account](/getting-started/create-account)** using Google.
3. **Map your five Cuttly roles onto four.** Cuttly has owner, admin, moderator, user and viewer. Trizlink has [owner, admin, member and viewer](/features/workspaces-and-teams#roles); a moderator or user usually becomes a member.
4. **Carry your UTM tags.** If you tagged links in Cuttly, save the recurring sets as [UTM templates](/features/tracking-and-utm#utm), so the spelling stays the same on every new link.
5. **Import the links, then rebuild each bio page.** Links go through the [importer](/features/link-organization#import). Pages do not import; rebuild each one from [blocks](/features/link-in-bio#blocks). Free covers 30 published pages.

## What stays on Cuttly {#stays}

- **cutt.ly addresses and your branded domains.** They keep resolving on Cuttly. Trizlink cannot [serve a custom domain](/features/custom-domains#not-live) yet.
- **TRAI headers and the team chat.** Neither exists here.
- **Click history.** It stays in Cuttly's reports.
