Carrd is a one-page site builder that many people use as a bio page, priced per year by how many sites you run. For full control over a single page's design at nineteen dollars a year, or a page whose main job is a Stripe, Gumroad or Typeform embed, it is hard to beat. Trizlink is the closer fit if you want tap counts per block and a contact inbox without wiring a third party, with the page beside your short links and social drafts. See [Trizlink and Carrd](https://trizlink.com/compare/carrd).

## Carrd site to bio page {#steps}

1. **Decide if it is really a bio page.** A Carrd site with several sections and a payment embed is closer to a website. A Carrd that is a stack of buttons is a bio page, and that is what moves well.
2. **[Sign in](/getting-started/create-account)** and pick the [layout](/features/link-in-bio#layouts) closest to your Carrd: Minimal for a column of links, Spotlight for one thing first.
3. **Rebuild with blocks, not code.** Buttons become Link blocks; text sections become Text and Heading blocks. A Custom HTML block exists, so test any embed in a draft before you rely on it.
4. **Replace the form provider.** If Carrd sent sign-ups to a mailing-list provider, the Email collection block keeps them in Trizlink instead. Contact messages go to an inbox here, not to a third party.
5. **[Publish](/features/link-in-bio#publishing)** at `trizlink.com/<slug>` and change the link in your profiles.

## What stays on Carrd {#stays}

- **Your custom domain.** Bio pages here are always on `trizlink.com`, and custom domains [do not serve traffic yet](/features/custom-domains#not-live).
- **Search visibility.** A Trizlink bio page is [not prerendered](/features/link-in-bio#privacy), so a crawler that skips JavaScript sees little. If search traffic to that page matters, read that section before you switch.
- **Your mailing-list subscribers.** They are with your provider, not with Carrd or Trizlink.
