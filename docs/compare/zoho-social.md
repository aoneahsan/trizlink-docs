Zoho's scheduler, with a free tier, low annual prices and add-on brands and members. This page is for you if you are on Zoho Social now and want to see what moving takes. For who each product suits better, read [Trizlink and Zoho Social](https://trizlink.com/compare/zoho-social).

## Moving across today {#steps}

**Publishing to social networks is built and switched off in Trizlink, so keep Zoho Social for anything that has to go out.** Read [what is not live yet](/features/social-media#not-live) first. Here is what you can set up beside it now.

1. **[Create a Trizlink account](/getting-started/create-account).** Sign-in is with Google, and a workspace is made for you.
2. **[Invite your team](/features/workspaces-and-teams#inviting).** Roles are owner, admin, member and viewer. There is no client role.
3. **Write in the [composer](/features/social-media#composer) and line posts up in the [queue](/features/social-media#queue).** Queued posts stay pending and nothing is sent.
4. **Move the links first.** [Short links](/features/short-links) and [bio pages](/features/link-in-bio) work today, and they are the part of Trizlink you can use while Zoho Social keeps posting.

## What does not come with you {#stays}

- **Your scheduled posts, connected accounts and post history.** They stay with Zoho Social. There is no importer for them.
- **Post approval.** Trizlink has no approval step before a post goes out.
- **TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest.** They cannot be connected. See [which platforms exist](/features/social-media#platforms).
