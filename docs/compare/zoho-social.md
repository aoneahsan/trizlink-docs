Zoho Social is Zoho's scheduler, with a free tier, low annual prices and add-on brands and members. If you already run Zoho CRM or Campaigns and want social inside that suite, or ten dollars a month on an annual plan is the budget, Zoho Social is the natural pick. Trizlink suits someone whose short links need their own reports and passwords, and whose members should come with the plan rather than at ten dollars each. [Trizlink and Zoho Social](https://trizlink.com/compare/zoho-social).

## Alongside Zoho Social {#steps}

**Zoho Social keeps posting, and your CRM link stays intact.** Trizlink publishes nothing today; [publishing is built and switched off](/features/social-media#not-live).

1. **[Sign in to Trizlink](/getting-started/create-account)** with the Google account your team uses.
2. **Add your teammates to the workspace.** [Roles](/features/workspaces-and-teams#roles) are owner, admin, member and viewer, on every plan.
3. **Rebuild your Zoho Social bio page** as a [Trizlink bio page](/features/link-in-bio#blocks) if you want it measured beside your links.
4. **Tag links the way your CRM expects.** Save the tags as [UTM templates](/features/tracking-and-utm#utm), so campaign tags reach your CRM spelled the same way every time.

## What stays on Zoho Social {#stays}

- **The Zoho suite connection.**
- **Scheduled posts, brands and history.**
- **Approvals, if you use them.** Trizlink has no approval step.
