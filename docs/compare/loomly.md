Loomly is a calendar-first planner with approval workflows, link shorteners and an AI assistant on every tier. Unlimited calendars with approval roles on the first paid tier, and two-factor enforcement per calendar, are reasons to stay. If forty-nine dollars a month is the wrong first price for a small team, Trizlink starts free, and its short links carry passwords and expiry dates. [Trizlink and Loomly](https://trizlink.com/compare/loomly).

## Loomly's links, moved {#steps}

**Calendars and approvals stay in Loomly.** Trizlink has neither approvals nor live publishing; [publishing is built and switched off](/features/social-media#not-live).

1. **Stop using Loomly's shorteners for new posts.** Make the links in [Trizlink](/features/short-links) so each one keeps its own click report.
2. **[Sign in](/getting-started/create-account)** and add the people from your Loomly plan as members, or as viewers if they only read reports.
3. **Give time-limited posts time-limited links.** A link can [expire on a date](/features/short-links#expiry) and show your own message afterwards.
4. **Paste the links into the posts on your Loomly calendar.**

## What stays on Loomly {#stays}

- **Calendars, approval roles and two-factor rules per calendar.**
- **Every scheduled post and its past performance.**
- **Links its shorteners already made.**
