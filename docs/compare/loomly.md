A calendar-first planner with approval workflows, link shorteners and an AI assistant on every tier. This page is for you if you are on Loomly now and want to see what moving takes. For who each product suits better, read [Trizlink and Loomly](https://trizlink.com/compare/loomly).

## Moving across today {#steps}

**Publishing to social networks is built and switched off in Trizlink, so keep Loomly for anything that has to go out.** Read [what is not live yet](/features/social-media#not-live) first. Here is what you can set up beside it now.

1. **[Create a Trizlink account](/getting-started/create-account).** Sign-in is with Google, and a workspace is made for you.
2. **[Invite your team](/features/workspaces-and-teams#inviting).** Roles are owner, admin, member and viewer. There is no client role.
3. **Write in the [composer](/features/social-media#composer) and line posts up in the [queue](/features/social-media#queue).** Queued posts stay pending and nothing is sent.
4. **Move the links first.** [Short links](/features/short-links) and [bio pages](/features/link-in-bio) work today, and they are the part of Trizlink you can use while Loomly keeps posting.

## What does not come with you {#stays}

- **Your scheduled posts, connected accounts and post history.** They stay with Loomly. There is no importer for them.
- **Post approval.** Trizlink has no approval step before a post goes out.
- **TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest.** They cannot be connected. See [which platforms exist](/features/social-media#platforms).
