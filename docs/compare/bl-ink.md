BL.INK is an enterprise link and QR code manager, now part of Loftware and sold through a consultation. It earns its place on physical products: GS1 Digital Link QR codes tied to a supply chain, bought on a contract with an onboarding team. Trizlink is for one person or a small team who wants a price on the page, a free plan to start on today, and the links, the bio page and the social drafts in one place. More on [who suits which](https://trizlink.com/compare/bl-ink).

## When some links move off BL.INK {#steps}

1. **Keep product labelling on BL.INK.** Codes printed on products under GS1 Digital Link are part of your supply chain. Trizlink does not offer that standard, so those codes stay.
2. **Pick out the marketing links.** Campaign links, social links and anything not printed on a product are the ones worth moving. Ask your BL.INK contact how to get them out; its API is listed on its pages.
3. **[Create the workspace](/getting-started/create-account)** and [invite whoever manages links](/features/workspaces-and-teams#inviting) as a member, or as a viewer if they only read reports.
4. **Import them** through the [CSV importer](/features/link-organization#import), with `target_url` in every row.
5. **Choose error correction before printing new codes.** For a flyer, **M** is fine; for a laminated or curved surface, pick **H** in the [QR panel](/features/qr-codes#error-correction).

## What stays on BL.INK {#stays}

- **Codes on products already shipped.** They encode BL.INK addresses.
- **Your contract and onboarding.** Trizlink is self-serve with roles on every plan, and there is no account manager.
- **History and domains.** Click history does not import, and a custom domain can be [verified](/features/custom-domains#not-live) here but not served.
