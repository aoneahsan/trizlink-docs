Later is a visual scheduler priced by social sets, with a link-in-bio page and best-time suggestions built in. If Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest auto-publishing with a visual feed preview is your daily work, or you earn through affiliate links it generates, Later is the right tool. Trizlink suits someone who wants a standing free plan rather than a trial, with short links that carry passwords and expiry dates. [Trizlink and Later](https://trizlink.com/compare/later).

## Moving Later's bio page {#steps}

**Keep posting from Later.** Trizlink cannot publish today; its publishing is [built and switched off](/features/social-media#not-live), and TikTok and Pinterest cannot be connected at all.

1. **Rebuild the Later bio page.** Go through its links and recreate them as Link blocks on a [Trizlink bio page](/features/link-in-bio#blocks). An Instagram feed block can sit above them.
2. **[Sign in](/getting-started/create-account)** and [publish](/features/link-in-bio#publishing) the page at `trizlink.com/<slug>`.
3. **Swap the link in your Instagram bio** once the page looks right on your phone.
4. **Use passwords and expiry where Later had none.** A link for a limited offer can [stop on a date or after a number of clicks](/features/short-links#expiry).

## What stays on Later {#stays}

- **Scheduled posts, social sets and post history.**
- **Affiliate links Later generated.** Keep them where they are paid out.
- **Approvals on Growth.** Trizlink has no approval step.
