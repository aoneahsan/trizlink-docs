The large enterprise suite, priced per user, with inbox automation, listening and an AI assistant. This page is for you if you are on Hootsuite now and want to see what moving takes. For who each product suits better, read [Trizlink and Hootsuite](https://trizlink.com/compare/hootsuite).

## Moving across today {#steps}

**Publishing to social networks is built and switched off in Trizlink, so keep Hootsuite for anything that has to go out.** Read [what is not live yet](/features/social-media#not-live) first. Here is what you can set up beside it now.

1. **[Create a Trizlink account](/getting-started/create-account).** Sign-in is with Google, and a workspace is made for you.
2. **[Invite your team](/features/workspaces-and-teams#inviting).** Roles are owner, admin, member and viewer. There is no client role.
3. **Write in the [composer](/features/social-media#composer) and line posts up in the [queue](/features/social-media#queue).** Queued posts stay pending and nothing is sent.
4. **Move the links first.** [Short links](/features/short-links) and [bio pages](/features/link-in-bio) work today, and they are the part of Trizlink you can use while Hootsuite keeps posting.

## What does not come with you {#stays}

- **Your scheduled posts, connected accounts and post history.** They stay with Hootsuite. There is no importer for them.
- **Post approval.** Trizlink has no approval step before a post goes out.
- **TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest.** They cannot be connected. See [which platforms exist](/features/social-media#platforms).
