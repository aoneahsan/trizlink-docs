A phone-first bio site builder with a free plan and three small monthly tiers. This page is for you if you are on Milkshake now and want to see what moving takes. For who each product suits better, read [Trizlink and Milkshake](https://trizlink.com/compare/milkshake).

## Moving your page across {#steps}

1. **Write down what is on your Milkshake page.** There is no importer for a page from another product, so this is the list you build from.
2. **[Create a Trizlink account](/getting-started/create-account).** Sign-in is with Google, and a workspace is made for you.
3. **Build the page from [blocks](/features/link-in-bio#blocks).** Pick a [layout](/features/link-in-bio#layouts) first, then add the links, the email form and the contact form you need.
4. **[Publish it and choose its address](/features/link-in-bio#publishing).** Open it on a phone before you change anything else.
5. **Put the new address in your profiles.** Leave the Milkshake page up until the new one has been in place for a while, because old posts still point at it.

## What does not come with you {#stays}

- **Your subscribers and messages.** They stay with Milkshake. This guide has no import step for them.
- **Your page history.** Views and taps start counting on the new page. See [limits](/features/link-in-bio#limits) for how a page is counted.
