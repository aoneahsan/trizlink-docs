Milkshake is a phone-first bio site builder with a free plan and three small monthly tiers. If you build everything from your phone and want the cheapest paid tier in this group, or a Mailchimp or Google Sheets sync is all the email tooling you need, it is a good choice. Thirty days of page history on the free plan is short, though; Trizlink's free plan keeps 90, and it puts short links and a social calendar in the same place. Read [Trizlink and Milkshake](https://trizlink.com/compare/milkshake).

## From the Milkshake app {#steps}

1. **Go through your Milkshake site and note each part of it.** A Trizlink bio page is one scrolling page, so decide the order everything should sit in from top to bottom.
2. **[Sign in to Trizlink](/getting-started/create-account).** You can build on a phone in the browser, or in the [Android app](/getting-started/create-account).
3. **Turn each part into a few blocks.** A section with a photo and three links becomes an Image block, a Heading and three Link blocks.
4. **Keep sign-ups inside the product.** On Milkshake Pro, emails go to Sheets or Mailchimp. Trizlink's Email collection block keeps them in the workspace.
5. **[Publish](/features/link-in-bio#publishing)** and replace the link in your bio.

## What stays on Milkshake {#stays}

- **Subscribers already sent to Sheets or Mailchimp.** They live there.
- **Your Milkshake address, and a Pro+ custom domain.** Trizlink pages are on `trizlink.com`, and custom domains [do not serve yet](/features/custom-domains#not-live).
- **The 30 days of history on Free.** Trizlink starts counting from the first view.
