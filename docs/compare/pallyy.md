Pallyy schedules by social set, with a free plan, client approvals and link-in-bio pages. A small agency that wants client approvals and shared calendars on a twenty-five dollar plan, or prefers paying ten dollars per extra set, is well served. Trizlink is for the person whose links cannot wait, while drafts and the calendar can wait until publishing is switched on, and whose short links need their own click reports. [Trizlink and Pallyy](https://trizlink.com/compare/pallyy).

## Pallyy's bio pages first {#steps}

**Pallyy keeps posting and handles client approvals.** Trizlink has no approval step, and its publishing is [built and switched off](/features/social-media#not-live).

1. **Move each client's link-in-bio page.** Rebuild it as a [Trizlink bio page](/features/link-in-bio#blocks), in that client's own [workspace](/features/workspaces-and-teams#what-it-owns).
2. **[Sign in](/getting-started/create-account)** and invite the agency's three users.
3. **Duplicate a finished page** for the next client; the copy is a draft with all its blocks.
4. **Give each client a viewer seat** so they can read their own click reports.

## What stays on Pallyy {#stays}

- **Client approvals and shared calendars.**
- **Social sets, scheduled posts and history.**
- **Views on the old bio pages.**
