Lnk.Bio is a bio-link page with a free plan, a very cheap monthly tier and a one-time Unique tier. If you want to pay once and never see a renewal, or you rely on its Instagram grid mirror and hundreds of embed services, it serves you well. Trizlink suits someone who wants 30 bio pages and short links on one free account, reporting into one set of numbers. [Trizlink and Lnk.Bio](https://trizlink.com/compare/lnk-bio).

## Leaving Lnk.Bio {#steps}

1. **Weigh the Unique purchase.** If you paid once, there is no renewal to escape. Moving makes more sense for a second page, or for links you want measured with the rest.
2. **Keep Instagram and TikTok posting on Lnk.Bio.** Its Unique tier schedules to both; Trizlink's publishing is built and switched off, so nothing goes out from here.
3. **[Sign in to Trizlink](/getting-started/create-account)** and rebuild the page from [blocks](/features/link-in-bio#blocks). An Instagram feed block shows your posts on the page.
4. **Use short links inside the page.** A Link block can point at a Trizlink [short link](/features/short-links), so the tap is counted on the page and the click on the link.
5. **[Publish](/features/link-in-bio#publishing)** and swap the address in your bio.

## What stays on Lnk.Bio {#stays}

- **Newsletter subscribers and contact messages** collected on Unique.
- **Scheduled Instagram and TikTok posts.**
- **The page's history.** Views on the new page count from zero.
