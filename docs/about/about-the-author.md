Trizlink is built and maintained by **the Trizlink team**. This page says who to reach and where, and points at
the pages that explain how the product works.

## About Trizlink

Trizlink is a web and Android link-management platform: short links with QR codes, bio pages, click
analytics, workspaces with roles, custom domains, campaign tracking, embeddable widgets, social publishing
and a public API.

It is built with React and TypeScript on the web, Capacitor for the Android app, and Supabase on Postgres for
the backend, with row-level security on every table. Sign-in is Google only.

Social publishing is complete and switched off because the developer applications behind it do not exist yet.
That is stated plainly on its own page rather than left for you to discover.

If you want to understand the product, start with the [Introduction](../intro.md), then the
[Quick start](../getting-started/quick-start.md).
