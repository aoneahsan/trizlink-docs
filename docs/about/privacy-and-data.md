This page is a plain-language summary of how TrizLink handles your data. It is not a substitute for the legal text — the authoritative document is the [Privacy Policy](https://trizlink.com/privacy-policy) on the product site, along with the [Terms](https://trizlink.com/terms), [Cookie Policy](https://trizlink.com/cookie-policy), [Data Security](https://trizlink.com/data-security), and [Data Deletion](https://trizlink.com/data-deletion) pages. Where this summary and the Privacy Policy differ, the Privacy Policy governs.

## What TrizLink stores

- **Account profile** — your name, email, and avatar from Google sign-in.
- **Your content** — the short links, link-in-bio pages, labels, templates, and settings you create.
- **Analytics** — click events for your links (country, referrer, device, browser, OS) so the [Analytics](/features/analytics) views work.
- **Preferences** — your [theme customizer](/features/theme-customizer) choices and notification settings.

This data is tied to your account and your active workspace. People only see a workspace's contents if you [invite them](/features/workspaces-and-teams).

## How sign-in works

TrizLink uses Google sign-in only and stores the basic Google profile it returns. There is no email/password credential held by TrizLink. See [Create your account](/getting-started/create-account).

## Analytics and third parties

TrizLink records link click events to power your dashboards, and uses product-analytics providers to understand how the app itself is used and to keep it reliable. The specific providers, what they receive, and your choices are listed in the product [Privacy Policy](https://trizlink.com/privacy-policy). TrizLink does not sell your links or your analytics.

If you turn on optional [AI features](/features/ai-features-byok), the content you choose to process is sent to your own AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) using the API key you supply. That request is governed by your agreement with that provider.

## The mobile app and permissions

The Android app requests only the permissions it needs for the features you use — for example, the camera for QR scanning and optional, off-by-default approximate location for analytics enrichment. Each permission is explained in the product Privacy Policy and is requested in context, not at launch.

## Deleting your account and data

- **Account deletion**: open **Settings → Danger Zone** and request deletion. It is scheduled with a 30-day grace period so you can cancel.
- **Data deletion request**: follow [trizlink.com/data-deletion](https://trizlink.com/data-deletion).

## Honest limits of this page

- This page summarizes; it does not list every provider, retention period, or legal basis — the [Privacy Policy](https://trizlink.com/privacy-policy) does.
- Analytics are best-effort. Click attribution depends on what the visitor's browser and network expose, so figures are indicative, not forensic.
