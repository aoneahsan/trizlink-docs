This changelog tracks notable updates to the TrizLink documentation site and the product areas it covers. For the in-app "What's New" and Play Store release notes, see the product at [trizlink.com](https://trizlink.com).

## 2026-06-22 — Documentation site launch

- Published the first version of the TrizLink documentation site at `docs.trizlink.com`.
- Wrote the [Introduction](/intro), a [Create your account](/getting-started/create-account) guide, and a [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start).
- Documented all current feature areas: [short links](/features/short-links), [link-in-bio](/features/link-in-bio), [QR codes](/features/qr-codes), [analytics](/features/analytics), [custom domains](/features/custom-domains), [link organization](/features/link-organization), [tracking & UTM](/features/tracking-and-utm), [workspaces & teams](/features/workspaces-and-teams), [social media](/features/social-media), [widgets](/features/widgets), [AI features (BYOK)](/features/ai-features-byok), [API access](/features/api-access), [utility tools](/features/utility-tools), the [browser extension](/features/browser-extension), the [theme customizer](/features/theme-customizer), and [sharing](/features/sharing).
- Added an [FAQ](/guides/faq) and a [Privacy & data](/about/privacy-and-data) summary.
- Wired SEO and AI-citability: robots.txt with an AI-bot allowlist, an `llms.txt` index, JSON-LD (WebSite, Organization, SoftwareApplication), and a generated sitemap.

_This documentation reflects the TrizLink product as of June 2026 (app version 2.0.1). Feature details are kept honest against the live app; if something here differs from what you see in the app, the app is authoritative._
