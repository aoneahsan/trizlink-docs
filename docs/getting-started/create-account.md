Creating a TrizLink account means signing in with your Google account — there is no separate email-and-password registration step. The first time you sign in, TrizLink creates your profile and a default workspace to hold your links, bio pages, and analytics. From there you can rename the workspace, invite teammates, or create more workspaces for different brands or clients.

## What you need

- A Google account (personal or Workspace).
- A modern browser, or the [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trizlink.app), or the [browser extension](/features/browser-extension).

That is the whole list. TrizLink is free to use and does not ask for a credit card.

## Use cases

- **A solo creator** signs in with a personal Google account and uses the default workspace for all their links.
- **A freelancer** creates one workspace per client so each client's links and analytics stay separate.
- **A small team** has the owner sign in, create a shared workspace, and [invite members](/features/workspaces-and-teams) with editor or viewer roles.
- **A returning user** signs in on a new device; their links and [theme preferences](/features/theme-customizer) follow them because everything is tied to the account.

## How it works

1. Go to [trizlink.com](https://trizlink.com) and choose **Sign in** (or open the Android app or extension).
2. Click **Continue with Google** and pick the Google account you want to use.
3. Approve the standard Google sign-in consent. TrizLink receives your name, email, and avatar to create your profile.
4. On first sign-in, a default workspace is created for you and you land on the dashboard.
5. Open **Settings → Workspaces** to rename the workspace or create additional ones.
6. (Optional) Open **Settings** to set notification preferences and your default workspace.

## Tips

- Use a Google account you will keep long-term, since it is the only way to sign in.
- Create a separate workspace before you start adding links if you manage more than one brand — moving links later is more work than starting clean.
- Set your default workspace in Settings so the dashboard always opens where you expect.
- If you also use the [browser extension](/features/browser-extension), sign in there too so shortened links sync to the same account.

## Managing and deleting your account

TrizLink keeps your profile, links, bio pages, analytics, and preferences tied to your account. You stay in control of all of it:

- **Account deletion** is available in **Settings → Danger Zone**. Deletion is scheduled with a 30-day grace period, so you can cancel if you change your mind.
- **Data deletion** details and a request path are documented on the product site at [trizlink.com/data-deletion](https://trizlink.com/data-deletion).
- See [Privacy & data](/about/privacy-and-data) for what is stored and how, and the product's [Privacy Policy](https://trizlink.com/privacy-policy) for the full legal text.

## FAQ

### Can I sign up with email and password instead of Google?

No. Sign-in is Google-only by design. If you do not have a Google account, you will need to create one to use TrizLink.

### Does creating an account cost anything?

No. TrizLink is free to use and does not ask for payment to create an account.

### What information does TrizLink get from Google?

The standard sign-in profile: your name, email address, and avatar. That is used to create and identify your account.

### Can I have more than one workspace?

Yes. You start with one default workspace and can create more from **Settings → Workspaces** — useful for separating brands or clients.

### How do I delete my account?

Open **Settings → Danger Zone** and request deletion. It is scheduled with a 30-day grace period before the data is removed, and you can cancel during that window.

### Is my data shared with other users?

No. Your links, bios, and analytics belong to you and your workspace. Only people you explicitly invite to a workspace can see its contents, and only at the role you give them.
