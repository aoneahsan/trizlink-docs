## Is the source code included with a licence?

No. A licence is a hosted deployment under your name. Code changes hands only with a buyout.

## Can another company license Trizlink at the same time?

Yes, until it is bought outright. After a buyout nobody new can license it, and licences sold before then keep
their terms.

## What does a buyout hand over?

The code of every repository that builds the web app and the Android app, the build configuration, and six months
of updates. It does not include the accounts or data of the people using trizlink.com; a buyout transfers code,
not users.

## Is there anything to pay after a buyout?

Yes, a royalty of 7% of the gross revenue Trizlink earns, leaving out any sales tax or VAT the buyer collects. It is
paid every quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as Trizlink earns revenue, and it is still owed after a
resale.

## How is a licence paid for?

By Wise bank transfer from our payment page. The licence is issued once the Trizlink team has confirmed the
transfer.

## What happens to the pages after a buyout?

They say the product has been acquired, and nothing is offered for sale. The verify page keeps working for every
licence sold before then.

## Can a deployment be changed for us alone?

Yes, as custom work: a different sign-in, an integration, a report in your format. It is quoted and paid on its
own. [Ask about custom work](https://trizlink.com/white-label/custom-work).

## Where are the prices?

On the [pricing page](https://trizlink.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them from the licensing service each
time it loads. This documentation does not repeat them, so it cannot fall out of date.
