Every licence and every buyout includes six months of updates from the day of payment.

## What counts as an update

- Fixes and security patches.
- Features shipped to Trizlink in that period.
- New Android builds from the same code.

A change made only for one buyer is not an update; it is
[custom work](https://trizlink.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted on its own.

## When the period ends

The deployment keeps running on the version it has. Nothing is switched off and nothing is removed.

## Renewing

A renewal adds 3, 6 or 12 months. It is counted from the later of two days: the day you renew, or the day your
current updates end. So renewing early loses nothing, and renewing late starts from the renewal.

Renew on the [updates page](https://trizlink.com/white-label/updates) with the licence key and the email address
the licence was issued to. A key and an address that do not belong together get the same answer as a key that
does not exist, so a key cannot be used to find out whose it is. Renewal prices are on that page.
