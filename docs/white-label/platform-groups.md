A licence covers the platform groups paid for. Trizlink ships two today, so those are the two on offer.

## Web app

The whole web product at the buyer's domain: short links with their QR codes and click reports, bio pages,
social drafts and the calendar, analytics, workspaces with their four roles, the public API, and the admin panel
the buyer's own team uses to run it. Sign-in pages, emails and every screen carry the buyer's name, logo and
colours.

## Mobile app (Android and iOS)

The Android app, built from the same code as the web app and published from the buyer's own Google Play account,
with the buyer's icon and name. Store accounts are the buyer's, and so are their fees.

iOS is part of this group, built when the buyer provides or pays for an Apple developer account. Until then the
mobile group delivers Android. Trizlink itself has no iOS app today.

## What is not a group

There is no browser extension and no desktop app, so neither can be licensed. A group appears on the
[pricing page](https://trizlink.com/white-label/pricing) only when the product ships it.
