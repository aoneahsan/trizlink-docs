Use Trizlink on your phone

By The Trizlink team

How do I use Trizlink on my phone?​

In the signed-in phone app view, use the bottom tabs for Dashboard, Links, Bio pages and Analytics. The top-left navigation button opens the rest of your workspace; New link sits above the bar on Dashboard and Links. You can reach the four main pages without reopening the full drawer each time.

On the web, this signed-in view appears only at 767 px or narrower with a coarse pointer; the Android app selects it regardless of those web conditions. Shrinking a desktop window alone does not turn it on. Public pages have no bottom tabs.

Four tabs only. If this is your first visit, the product walkthrough shows the main screens at phone size before you make anything.

How do I move between the main pages?​

Tap a bottom tab. Its label matches the page name in the workspace navigation. The selected tab stays marked while you open a page beneath it, so a link's edit form still shows Links as selected. Tap that tab to return to the list.

Where are the other workspace pages?​

Press the navigation button at the top left. The drawer has the same workspace pages as the wide-screen sidebar, including Social and Domains. The workspace switch sits above the list. Pick a page; the drawer closes. The top-right account button opens account choices separately from the page list.

How do I make a short link from the phone view?​

On Dashboard or Links, press New link above the bottom bar; if you can create links in the workspace, the page opens the new-link form for your destination. Without that permission, the action stays visible but the page shows a read-only notice. No form appears.

The thumb action is absent on Bio pages, Analytics and other workspace pages. Return to Dashboard or Links.
